BiographyBiographie

I am an economist working at the meeting point of three fields: macroeconomics, computational economics and mathematical economics. Macroeconomics supplies the questions, computational economics the means of answering them once the models stop admitting closed-form solutions, and mathematical economics the discipline that keeps the answers well posed. The combination is what allows me to move between a theoretical model, its numerical resolution and an estimate taken to the data without changing craft along the way.

Je suis économiste et je travaille au point de rencontre de trois champs : la macroéconomie, l’économie computationnelle et l’économie mathématique. La macroéconomie fournit les questions, l’économie computationnelle les moyens d’y répondre lorsque les modèles cessent d’admettre une solution analytique, et l’économie mathématique la rigueur qui garde les réponses bien posées. Cette combinaison me permet de passer d’un modèle théorique à sa résolution numérique puis à une estimation confrontée aux données sans changer de métier en chemin.

How the work is organisedL’organisation du travail

I work on both sides of macroeconomics. On the theoretical side, I build DSGE and overlapping-generations models to trace how households, policy and external constraints interact over the life cycle. On the empirical side, I take those mechanisms to the data with panel and time-varying-parameter methods. Each side informs the other, since a model indicates which correlations should appear in the data, and the estimates then discipline the next model.

Je travaille des deux côtés de la macroéconomie. Du côté théorique, je construis des modèles DSGE et à générations imbriquées pour suivre l’interaction entre les ménages, la politique économique et les contraintes extérieures sur le cycle de vie. Du côté empirique, je confronte ces mécanismes aux données par des méthodes de panel et à paramètres variables dans le temps. Chaque versant éclaire l’autre, puisque le modèle indique quelles corrélations devraient apparaître dans les données et que les estimations disciplinent ensuite le modèle suivant.

Four strands organise that effort. The first is monetary and fiscal policy, where I ask how the policy rate and the public budget transmit to output, prices and the external accounts, and what limits that transmission once an economy is open to trade and to global financial conditions. The second looks at small open economies under partial dollarization, using the Democratic Republic of Congo as a working laboratory for how external monetary shocks travel through exchange rates, commodity prices and reserves. The third concerns financial factors in macroeconomic modelling and the welfare costs of inflation, the subject of my work on markups and trend inflation. The fourth, and the longest-running, applies computable general equilibrium analysis to the Congolese economy.

Quatre axes structurent cet effort. Le premier porte sur les politiques monétaire et budgétaire : j’examine comment le taux directeur et le budget public se transmettent à la production, aux prix et aux comptes extérieurs, et ce qui limite cette transmission dès lors qu’une économie s’ouvre aux échanges et aux conditions financières mondiales. Le deuxième porte sur les petites économies ouvertes partiellement dollarisées, avec la République démocratique du Congo comme laboratoire pour comprendre la transmission des chocs monétaires externes par les taux de change, les prix des matières premières et les réserves. Le troisième porte sur les facteurs financiers dans la modélisation macroéconomique et sur les coûts en bien-être de l’inflation, objet de mes travaux sur les markups et la tendance inflationniste. Le quatrième, le plus ancien, applique l’analyse en équilibre général calculable à l’économie congolaise.

Artificial Intelligence, Aging, and the Macroeconomy: A Life-Cycle General Equilibrium Model with Endogenous Fertility, AI Capital, and Longevity

Single-authoredAuteur unique

This paper develops a general equilibrium overlapping-generations model with endogenous fertility, in which firms accumulate both physical and artificial intelligence capital, and uses it to study the macroeconomic transmission of two structural disturbances, an AI technology shock and a longevity shock. The AI shock acts as a capital demand disturbance. It raises all rates of return, most sharply the return to AI capital, reallocates investment from physical to AI capital, and produces a frontloaded output expansion that decays monotonically. The longevity shock acts as a saving supply disturbance. It deepens the aggregate capital stock, compresses returns and the real interest rate, and generates hump-shaped, persistent dynamics. The two shocks move fertility in opposite directions, since AI raises it modestly through an income effect while longevity lowers it by strengthening the life-cycle saving motive and the cost of child-rearing. A forecast-error variance decomposition attributes most aggregate volatility to the longevity shock, while the AI shock dominates the variance of the return to AI capital. Fertility is strongly countercyclical and almost perfectly negatively correlated with hours worked, placing household time allocation at the centre of the mechanism. A robustness analysis confirms that these conclusions reflect structural properties of the model. Variation in the capital share and in the persistence of the AI shock leaves the signs of the wage, fertility, output and consumption responses unchanged, and only the elasticity of substitution between labour and AI can reverse them, beyond a threshold well above standard empirical estimates.

Transmission of U.S. Monetary Policy Shocks to a Dollarized, Resource-Dependent Economy: Evidence from the Democratic Republic of Congo

Single-authoredAuteur unique

This paper explores the dynamic effects of U.S. monetary tightening on macroeconomic and financial conditions in the Democratic Republic of Congo, a small, resource-dependent and partially dollarized economy. Using a time-varying parameter vector autoregression (TVP-VAR), the study traces the responses of the Congolese franc, copper prices, inflation, broad money and international reserves to an unexpected increase in the U.S. policy rate in January 2019, which coincided with a new political regime. The initial shock induces currency depreciation, a decline in copper prices, short-term disinflation, a contraction in M2 and a fall in reserves. Later dynamics include delayed appreciation, recovery in copper prices and adjustment in monetary aggregates, pointing to compensatory capital flows, export revenues and central bank intervention. The results show how exposed the country is to external financial shocks, how much weight domestic monetary and exchange rate policy carries, and how political regime shifts affect the propagation of those shocks.

International Monetary Policy and Exchange Rate Dynamics in Dollarized Economies: Evidence from the DR Congo

withavec Jean Blaise Nlemfu Mukoko

This paper examines the transmission of external monetary disturbances in a highly dollarized small open economy, using the Democratic Republic of Congo as a representative case. We develop a tractable open-economy model with partial dollarization, incomplete price adjustment and an endogenous risk premium linked to interest rate differentials. Simulations indicate that a U.S. interest rate tightening generates an immediate and persistent depreciation of the domestic currency, alongside rising risk premia and inflationary pressure. Money supply shocks amplify exchange rate and price responses under dollarization as real money demand weakens, while risk premium shocks add volatility through expectation-driven dynamics. Across shock types, domestic monetary policy has limited capacity to offset external disturbances. Dollarization changes how shocks spread, since it reduces policy independence and magnifies the weight of global financial conditions, so stabilization requires tools beyond the interest rate.

International Migration, Ageing, and External Imbalances: A Dynamic Analysis with a Two-Country Life-Cycle Economy

withavec Kevin Moran

This paper develops a two-country, open-economy quantitative model to analyse the macroeconomic implications of demographic shifts such as population ageing, declining population growth, increased longevity and international migration. The model builds on the overlapping-generations framework of Gertler (1999), with young and old households in each country, and calibrates entry, retirement and exit rates to observed population growth, retirement ages and life expectancy. It accommodates differential population growth across countries, migration from developing to developed economies, divergent productivity and imperfect global financial integration. Simulations focus on how ageing, migration and productivity differences affect net exports and the current account in two regions representing the global North and the global South. Demographic trends emerge as a main driver of aggregate savings and external balances. Demographic shocks produce trade and current account surpluses in the North, while productivity differences shape investment patterns across regions.

Demographic Trends, International Migration, and External Imbalances: A Regional Analysis

withavec Kevin Moran

This paper presents an empirical analysis of how global demographic change and international migration affect external balances, grouping countries into advanced, emerging and developing economies and proceeding region by region. Panel models estimate the impact of one demographic factor at a time, whether ageing, fertility, life expectancy or immigration, on one macroeconomic aggregate at a time, such as consumption, savings, investment, the trade balance or the current account. Larger shares of older adults weigh on the trade balance in advanced and emerging economies. Ageing is associated with lower savings rates and current account ratios in emerging and developing economies, while in advanced economies savings tend to rise with life expectancy, ageing and net migration. Net immigration raises savings rates in advanced economies and investment in emerging and developing ones. The results underline why regional conditioning matters, since all regions face demographic change but not at the same scale.

The Effects of Demographic Changes and International Migration on Economic Growth

withavec Kevin Moran

This paper analyses empirically how global demographic change affects GDP growth, region by region across advanced, emerging and developing economies. A decomposition of per capita GDP growth shows that hourly productivity and total hours worked drive growth in advanced economies, while hourly productivity and an expanding working-age population matter most in emerging economies. Falling hours per job have weighed on per capita growth in advanced economies. Panel estimates then take one demographic factor at a time. Larger shares of older adults raise per capita growth in emerging and developing economies but reduce it in advanced ones. Shifts in the working-age population raise per capita GDP across all groups, while a larger youth share lowers it. Net immigration raises per capita growth in advanced and developing economies and lowers it in emerging ones. Taken together, the estimates show that the macroeconomic consequences of demographic shifts depend on the stage of development.

Fertility Decisions, International Migration, Remittances, and Global Imbalances in a Two-Country Life-Cycle Economy

Single-authoredAuteur unique

Using a two-country life-cycle framework with endogenous fertility and remittances, this paper investigates how positive longevity, immigration and productivity shocks affect external imbalances in the North and the South. Positive demographic and productivity shocks improve the North’s trade balance, with demographic shocks the primary driver, followed by productivity, immigration and longevity. Rising productivity and immigration in the North lower the South’s trade balance, while a longevity shock in the North raises it. Longevity, immigration and productivity shocks drive the negative dynamics of the North’s current account, whereas demographic shocks improve the South’s net foreign asset position and current account. Higher longevity in the North raises the old-age dependency ratio, net migration and the fertility of foreign-born workers. Higher immigration raises foreign-born fertility and population growth while lowering native-born fertility. A positive productivity shock lowers fertility, population growth and the old-age dependency ratio.

The accounts of an open, mineral-dependent economyLes comptes d’une économie ouverte et minière

What follows sets out the Congolese accounts in the order an open-economy macroeconomist reads them: the level of activity first, then the external constraint that acts on it. The two are tied together by a single mechanism. Mineral exports carry the trade balance; a structural deficit on services, freight above all, takes back a good part of what the mines bring in; and what remains, once foreign direct investment and debt service have passed through, is the room the country actually has. The tables give the published figures for each of those steps and state plainly where a published figure does not exist.

Ce qui suit dispose les comptes congolais dans l’ordre où les lit un macroéconomiste de l’économie ouverte : le niveau d’activité d’abord, puis la contrainte extérieure qui s’exerce sur lui. Un seul mécanisme les relie. Les exportations minières portent la balance commerciale ; un déficit structurel sur les services, le fret au premier chef, reprend une bonne part de ce que les mines rapportent ; et ce qui subsiste, une fois passés l’investissement direct étranger et le service de la dette, constitue la marge dont le pays dispose réellement. Les tableaux donnent les chiffres publiés pour chacune de ces étapes et disent clairement là où aucun chiffre publié n’existe.

The Congolese budget collects about fourteen per cent of output and spends about seventeen, and between a third and a half of what it collects comes out of the ground. That single proportion is why the fiscal stance in the DRC is not chosen so much as received: it moves with the copper and cobalt cycle, and the deficit widens when the cycle turns. The monetary table alongside records the disinflation of 2023 to 2025 and the exchange rate that accompanied it, together with a dollarisation ratio around ninety per cent which is what limits the transmission of any policy rate the central bank sets.

Le budget congolais prélève quelque quatorze pour cent de la production et en dépense environ dix-sept, et entre le tiers et la moitié de ce qu’il prélève sort du sol. Reading the price cycleLecture du cycle des prix

Three features of this cycle are unusual and each has a distinct policy implication. The first is that the metals boom is broad. Copper is up 36 per cent on its 2025 average, tin 56 per cent, zinc 25 per cent, and the World Bank metals and minerals index has moved from 112 to 141 on its 2010 base. This is not a copper-specific event that a single mine expansion could explain; it is a demand and inventory cycle in industrial metals as a class, and it will turn.

Trois traits de ce cycle sont inhabituels et chacun appelle une conclusion distincte pour la politique économique. Le premier est que la hausse des métaux est générale. Le cuivre progresse de 36 pour cent sur sa moyenne de 2025, l’étain de 56 pour cent, le zinc de 25 pour cent, et l’indice des métaux et minéraux de la Banque mondiale est passé de 112 à 141 sur sa base 2010. Il ne s’agit pas d’un événement propre au cuivre qu’une seule extension de mine expliquerait : c’est un cycle de demande et de stocks portant sur les métaux industriels comme classe, et il se retournera.

The second is that two of the four prices that matter most to the Congolese budget are now set, in part, in Kinshasa. Cobalt hydroxide had fallen to 5.60 dollars a pound when the export suspension of 22 February 2025 was announced; it stood at 25.40 dollars in June 2026 under the quota regime. That is a quadrupling produced by a supply decision rather than by demand, and it is the clearest recent demonstration that a country holding 73 per cent of world production has market power. Tantalite rose 43 per cent in seven weeks after the Rubaya landslide, for the opposite reason: an involuntary supply interruption in the east. The same mechanism, one voluntary and one not, tells the government what its share of world supply is worth, and warns it that a price obtained by restricting volume is a revenue gain only while the elasticity of substitution stays low.

Le deuxième est que deux des quatre prix qui comptent le plus pour le budget congolais se forment désormais, en partie, à Kinshasa. L’hydroxyde de cobalt était tombé à 5,60 dollars la livre lorsque la suspension des exportations du 22 février 2025 a été annoncée ; il valait 25,40 dollars en juin 2026 sous le régime de quotas. C’est un quadruplement produit par une décision d’offre et non par la demande, et c’est la démonstration récente la plus nette qu’un pays détenant 73 pour cent de la production mondiale dispose d’un pouvoir de marché. La tantalite a bondi de 43 pour cent en sept semaines après l’éboulement de Rubaya, pour la raison inverse : une interruption involontaire de l’offre dans l’est. Le même mécanisme, volontaire dans un cas et subi dans l’autre, indique au gouvernement ce que vaut sa part de l’offre mondiale, et l’avertit qu’un prix obtenu en restreignant les volumes n’est un gain de recettes que tant que l’élasticité de substitution reste faible.

The third is that the import side has turned against the country at the same time. Brent averaged 69 dollars in 2025 and 104 in the second quarter of 2026, a rise of more than a half at the peak. With an import dependence of 0.96 on refined fuels, that increase passes almost entirely into the Congolese price level. The favourable terms-of-trade shock on the export side is therefore substantially offset on the import side, which is why the current account improvement has been smaller than the copper price alone would suggest.

Le troisième est que le côté importations s’est retourné contre le pays au même moment. Le Brent a valu 69 dollars en moyenne en 2025 et 104 au deuxième trimestre de 2026, soit plus de la moitié de hausse au sommet. Which filter, and why these parametersQuel filtre, et pourquoi ces paramètres

A cycle is not observed; it is what remains once a trend has been removed, and the trend is a choice. Reporting a single detrending would hide that choice, so the series are passed through five estimators and the results are shown side by side. What is asked of them is not that they agree on the size of the gap — they do not, the standard deviation of the output cycle runs from 0.2 to 2.6 per cent depending on the filter — but that they agree on the dates of the turns and on the ordering of the volatilities. They do.

Un cycle ne s’observe pas : il est ce qui reste une fois la tendance retirée, et la tendance est un choix. Ne publier qu’un seul détrendage reviendrait à masquer ce choix ; les séries sont donc passées par cinq estimateurs et les résultats sont montrés côte à côte. Ce qu’on leur demande n’est pas de s’accorder sur l’ampleur de l’écart — ils ne s’accordent pas, l’écart-type du cycle de la production va de 0,2 à 2,6 pour cent selon le filtre — mais de s’accorder sur la date des retournements et sur le classement des volatilités. Ils s’accordent.

The smoothing parameter of the Hodrick–Prescott filter is set at λ = 14400. It is not a free number. Hodrick and Prescott (1997) calibrated λ = 1600 on quarterly United States data, as the price at which a trend is allowed to bend. Backus and Kehoe (1992) carried that calibration to other frequencies by the square of the frequency ratio, which gives 1600 × 3² = 14400 for monthly data; Ravn and Uhlig (2002) redid the exercise and obtained the fourth power, 1600 × 3⁴ = 129600. The plate on the smoothing parameter shows all three, 1600, 14400 and 129600, on the same series, so that the reader can see what the choice buys and what it costs: a low value hands the movement to the trend and leaves a flat cycle, a high one holds the trend straight and pushes every swing into the cycle. The tables use 14400, the intermediate and the more common convention for monthly work, and the plate is there so that the convention is visible rather than implicit.

Le paramètre de lissage du filtre de Hodrick et Prescott est fixé à λ = 14400. Ce n’est pas un nombre libre. Hodrick et Prescott (1997) ont calibré λ = 1600 sur des données trimestrielles américaines, comme le prix auquel on autorise la tendance à se courber. Backus et Kehoe (1992) ont transporté ce calibrage aux autres fréquences par le carré du rapport de fréquence, ce qui donne 1600 × 3² = 14400 en mensuel ; Ravn et Uhlig (2002) ont refait l’exercice et obtenu la puissance quatrième, 1600 × 3⁴ = 129600. La planche consacrée au lissage montre les trois valeurs, 1600, 14400 et 129600, sur la même série, pour que le lecteur voie ce que le choix achète et ce qu’il coûte : une valeur faible confie le mouvement à la tendance et laisse un cycle plat, une valeur forte maintient la tendance droite et rejette toute l’oscillation dans le cycle. Les tableaux retiennent 14400, la valeur intermédiaire et la convention la plus répandue en mensuel, et la planche est là pour que cette convention soit visible au lieu d’être tacite.

The four other estimators answer four known objections to that filter. Hamilton (2018) objects that the Hodrick–Prescott cycle is a filtered artefact with no economic content, and proposes instead the residual of a regression of the series on its own value h periods earlier and p − 1 further lags; the two-year horizon he recommends, h = 8 quarters and p = 4, becomes h = 24 and p = 12 months here. Baxter and King (1999) and Christiano and Fitzgerald (2003) object that a trend should be defined by frequency and not by curvature, and keep only the movements whose period lies between eighteen and ninety-six months — that is between a year and a half and eight years, the band Burns and Mitchell fixed for the business cycle in 1946. The Baxter–King approximation is truncated at K = 36 months, which is the three-year window they recommend and which costs three years of data at each end of the sample; the Christiano–Fitzgerald filter is used in its asymmetric full-sample form, which loses no observation at the price of a weight that changes with the date. The first difference, finally, has no parameter at all: it is there as the benchmark against which the other four have to justify their machinery.

Les quatre autres estimateurs répondent à quatre objections connues faites à ce filtre. Hamilton (2018) objecte que le cycle de Hodrick et Prescott est un artefact de filtrage sans contenu économique, et propose à sa place le résidu d’une régression de la série sur sa propre valeur h périodes plus tôt et p − 1 retards supplémentaires ; l’horizon de deux ans qu’il recommande, h = 8 trimestres et p = 4, devient ici h = 24 et p = 12 mois. Baxter et King (1999) et Christiano et Fitzgerald (2003) objectent qu’une tendance devrait se définir par la fréquence et non par la courbure, et ne retiennent que les mouvements dont la période est comprise entre dix-huit et quatre-vingt-seize mois — soit entre un an et demi et huit ans, la bande que Burns et Mitchell ont fixée au cycle des affaires en 1946. L’approximation de Baxter et King est tronquée à K = 36 mois, la fenêtre de trois ans qu’ils recommandent, et qui coûte trois années de données à chaque extrémité de l’échantillon ; le filtre de Christiano et Fitzgerald est employé dans sa forme asymétrique en échantillon complet, qui ne perd aucune observation au prix d’une pondération qui change avec la date. La différence première, enfin, n’a aucun paramètre : elle est là comme étalon, celui devant lequel les quatre autres doivent justifier leur appareillage.

One warning belongs with these plates. Several of the monthly series are interpolated from annual publications, which the filters cannot know: the autocorrelation column of the comparison table therefore measures the interpolation as much as it measures the persistence of the Congolese economy, and should be read as an upper bound. What does survive every filter is the shape rather than the size: investment stays two and a half to three and a half times as volatile as output, consumption stays smoother than output, and both stay procyclical.

Un avertissement accompagne ces planches. Plusieurs des séries mensuelles sont interpolées à partir de publications annuelles, ce que les filtres ne peuvent pas savoir : la colonne d’autocorrélation du tableau de comparaison mesure donc l’interpolation autant que la persistance de l’économie congolaise, et doit se lire comme une borne supérieure. Ce qui survit à tous les filtres, c’est la forme plutôt que l’ampleur : l’investissement reste deux fois et demie à trois fois et demie plus volatil que la production, la consommation reste plus lisse que la production, et les deux restent procycliques.

ProcedureProcédure

How these panels are built. Each series is first put in the form a filter can take. A strictly positive series is replaced by a hundred times its natural logarithm, so that the deviation from trend reads directly as a percentage; a rate is already in percentage points and is filtered as it stands; a balance, which changes sign, admits no logarithm and is filtered in its own unit. The five filters are then applied to that same transformed series, and each panel shows the cyclical component one of them returns.Comment ces panneaux sont construits. Chaque série est d’abord mise sous la forme qu’un filtre peut prendre. Une série strictement positive est remplacée par cent fois son logarithme naturel, de sorte que l’écart à la tendance se lise directement en pourcentage ; un taux est déjà en points de pourcentage et se filtre tel quel ; un solde, qui change de signe, n’admet pas le logarithme et se filtre dans son unité propre. Les cinq filtres sont ensuite appliqués à cette même série transformée, et chaque panneau montre la composante cyclique que l’un d’eux restitue.

Hodrick-Prescott. The trend is the series that minimises the sum of squared deviations from the data plus λ times the sum of squared second differences of the trend itself; the cycle is what is left. The smoothing parameter is set to λ = 14400, the monthly counterpart of the quarterly 1600 under the fourth-power rule; the plate that follows shows what other values would have decided. The filter uses the whole sample and loses no observation, but the last points of the trend are estimated with data on one side only and move as the sample grows.Hodrick et Prescott. La tendance est la série qui minimise la somme des écarts au carré aux données, augmentée de λ fois la somme des carrés de ses propres différences secondes ; le cycle est ce qui reste. Le paramètre de lissage est fixé à λ = 14400, la contrepartie mensuelle du 1600 trimestriel selon la règle de la puissance quatre ; la planche suivante montre ce qu’auraient décidé d’autres valeurs. Le filtre utilise tout l’échantillon et ne perd aucune observation, mais les derniers points de la tendance sont estimés avec des données d’un seul côté et bougent à mesure que l’échantillon s’allonge.

Hamilton regression. The value observed h months ahead is regressed on a constant and on p successive lags of the series; the cycle is the residual of that regression, that is, the part of the future value that the recent past does not predict. The parameters are h = 24 and p = 12, the monthly transposition of the (8, 4) pair Hamilton recommends for quarterly data. The construction consumes the first h + p − 1 = 35 months, which is why this panel starts later than the others.Régression de Hamilton. On régresse la valeur observée h mois plus tard sur une constante et sur p retards successifs de la série ; le cycle est le résidu de cette régression, c’est-à-dire la part de la valeur future que le passé récent ne prédit pas. Les paramètres retenus sont h = 24 et p = 12, transposition mensuelle du couple (8, 4) que Hamilton recommande pour des données trimestrielles. La construction consomme les h + p − 1 = 35 premiers mois : c’est pourquoi ce panneau commence plus tard que les autres.

Band-pass filters. Both keep only the fluctuations whose period falls between 18 and 96 months — a year and a half to eight years, the range Burns and Mitchell assigned to the business cycle — and discard everything slower or faster. The Baxter-King filter is a symmetric moving average truncated at K = 36 months, which costs three years at each end of the sample; the Christiano-Fitzgerald filter is asymmetric, uses every observation and therefore loses none, at the price of weights that differ from one date to the next.Filtres passe-bande. Tous deux ne retiennent que les fluctuations dont la période tombe entre 18 et 96 mois — un an et demi à huit ans, l’intervalle que Burns et Mitchell assignaient au cycle des affaires — et rejettent tout ce qui est plus lent ou plus rapide. Le filtre de Baxter et King est une moyenne mobile symétrique tronquée à K = 36 mois, ce qui coûte trois ans à chaque extrémité de l’échantillon ; celui de Christiano et Fitzgerald est asymétrique, utilise toutes les observations et n’en perd donc aucune, au prix de pondérations qui diffèrent d’une date à l’autre.

First difference. The plainest cycle there is: the change from one month to the next, which removes any trend that is a straight line and keeps the rest. It loses only the first month. It is shown here because it assumes nothing, and because where it agrees with the other four the reading does not depend on the filter.Différence première. Le cycle le plus simple qui soit : la variation d’un mois à l’autre, qui retranche toute tendance linéaire et garde le reste. Elle ne perd que le premier mois. Elle figure ici parce qu’elle ne suppose rien, et parce que là où elle s’accorde avec les quatre autres, la lecture ne dépend pas du filtre.

Reading the panels. The vertical scale is free in each panel: the filters do not return comparable amplitudes, and forcing a common scale would flatten three of them. What is comparable, and what these plates are for, is the date of the turning points. Where the five panels turn together, the cycle is in the data; where they part, it is in the method. The shaded bands mark the episodes of macroeconomic stress, identical on every panel.Lire les panneaux. L’échelle verticale est libre dans chaque panneau : les filtres ne restituent pas des amplitudes comparables, et leur imposer une échelle commune en aplatirait trois. Ce qui est comparable, et ce à quoi ces planches servent, est la date des retournements. Là où les cinq panneaux se retournent ensemble, le cycle est dans les données ; là où ils divergent, il est dans la méthode. Les plages grisées signalent les épisodes de tension macroéconomique, identiques sur tous les panneaux.
