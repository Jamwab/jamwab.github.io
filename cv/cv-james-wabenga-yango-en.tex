% ======================= PROFILE ==========================================
\section{Profile}
I am an economist and a teacher. I hold a doctorate in economics from Université Laval (2025) and a master's degree in economics for development from the University of Oxford. My work sits at the meeting point of macroeconomics, computational economics and mathematical economics: the first supplies the questions, the second the means of answering them once the models stop admitting closed-form solutions, and the third the discipline that keeps the answers well posed. Since 2020 I have been a lecturer at Université Laval and at the Université du Québec à Chicoutimi, where I have taught some fifteen undergraduate and graduate courses, in French and in English: macroeconomics, microeconomics, international economics, international trade, managerial economics and quantitative methods. To keep the theory concrete I draw on modelling work carried out for a range of ministries and agencies -- the UNDP, the Congolese and Nigerien planning authorities, central banks -- so that the concepts connect to the policy questions that prompted them. Alongside the teaching I pursue research in macroeconomics on population ageing, international migration and monetary policy.
